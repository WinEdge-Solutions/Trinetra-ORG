Statutory & Emergency Disclaimer

Legal Disclosures under the Companies Act 2013 and Emergency Services Protocol

emergency Emergency Intake Limitation of Liability

Trinetra Gargi Foundation's website and Help & Report portal serve as an electronic communication and casework intake channel. The online portal does not replace direct government emergency services. In active, life-threatening crises, physical violence, or immediate child danger, citizens must dial 112 or 1098 directly.

Electronic submissions are triaged by available field staff and do not guarantee immediate on-scene response within a predetermined window.

1. Non-Governmental Entity Status

Trinetra Gargi Foundation is a licensed non-governmental non-profit entity (Section 8 Company under the Companies Act, 2013). We are not an official agency, department, or instrumentality of the Government of India or the Government of Rajasthan.

Information provided in our Government Schemes Directory is compiled for public civic education. Decisions regarding eligibility, quota allocation, or benefit disbursement are made solely by respective government authorities.
