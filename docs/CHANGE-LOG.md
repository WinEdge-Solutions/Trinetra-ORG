# Change Log & Revision Governance

---

## 1. Revision Protocol & Contractual Boundaries

Per quotation **TGF-2026-003**, the client is entitled to **two (2) included revision rounds** prior to official handover:
- **Round Definition:** A round consists of a single, consolidated written punch list submitted by the Foundation within three (3) working days of receiving the staging URL.
- **Classification Rules:**
  - **Defect (Fix):** Any element, link, form, or visual style that does not function as explicitly agreed in the PRD and page specs. **Cost: INR 0 (Included).**
  - **Change (Scope Addition):** Any design variation, new field, new page, content reorganization, or feature addition that differs from or exceeds the agreed scope. **Cost: Billed per Out-of-Scope Rate Card.**
