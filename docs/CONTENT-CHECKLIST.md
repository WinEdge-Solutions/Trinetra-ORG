## 2. Supply Verification Process

1. **Submission Format:** All textual content must be delivered in digital format (`.docx`, `.txt`, or directly formatted `.json`).
2. **Photography Standards:** High-resolution JPG or PNG assets. Must conform to [`SAFEGUARDING-AND-LEGAL.md`](SAFEGUARDING-AND-LEGAL.md) (no identifiable minor faces).
3. **Approval Protocol:** Each delivered item must be signed off by a designated Foundation officer before setting **Approved** to `Y` and toggling `PLACEHOLDER: false` in `/content`.
