Girl Child & Education Ministry of WCD

Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP)

National flagship initiative preventing gender-biased sex selection, securing birth survival, and guaranteeing free institutional secondary education for the girl child.

check_circle ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
  • • Indian female child from birth up to 18 years of age.
  • • Enrolled in recognized government or municipal schooling system.

Child Birth Certificate Parents Aadhaar School Bonafide
Trinetra Booth Aid: Our grassroots desk helps obtain delayed birth certificates through district revenue officers and facilitates formal school re-enrollment.

Girl Child & Financial Security Ministry of Finance

Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

High-interest, tax-free small deposit savings scheme for girl children, providing safe compound wealth creation for higher studies and matrimonial independence.

Current Interest Rate 8.2% p.a. (Govt Guaranteed)
Tenure / Maturity 21 Years from Opening
account_balance POST OFFICE / BANK SETUP GUIDE
  • • Account opened before child turns 10 years of age.
  • • Min. initial deposit ₹250; maximum ₹1,50,000 per financial year.
  • • 100% Tax-Exempt under Section 80C on deposits and maturity.
Child Birth Certificate Guardian KYC (PAN/Aadhaar) Address Proof

verified Official Scheme Poster Ministry of Education
Statutory Education Right Ministry of Education

RTE Section 12(1)(c) Mandate

Legally mandated 25% free seat reservation in non-minority private unaided schools for children from Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) and Disadvantaged Groups.

how_to_reg LOTTERY ADMISSION PROCESS
  • • Age window: 3-5 years (Nursery/KG) or 6 years for Class 1.
  • • State online centralized portal allocation via transparent lottery.
  • • Complete tuition, textbook, and uniform fee waiver until Class 8.
folder_open DOCUMENT CHECKLIST
Income Certificate (< ₹1 Lakh) Caste/Category Cert (if applicable) Ward/Neighborhood Proof
Trinetra Booth Aid: Online lottery application filing assistance, GPS neighborhood mapping, and legal grievance redressal if private schools deny admission.
National RTE Portal open_in_new support_agent RTE Application Filing

Youth Skill Development MSDE / NSDC

PM Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY 4.0)

Free industry-standard technical training, NSQF government certification, monetary stipend, and direct job placement support for dropouts and youth.

bolt KEY OFFERINGS
  • • 100% Free Short-Term Training (STT) across 30+ sectors (IT, Solar, Healthcare).
  • • Assessment & Certification reward with DBT conveyance stipend.
  • • Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) for experienced informal workers.

Age: 15–45 Years Aadhaar Linked Mobile Active Bank Account
Trinetra Booth Aid: We administer aptitude assessments, match candidates to vetted Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Kendras (PMKK), and oversee placement compliance.

Child Welfare & Nutrition Ministry of WCD

Poshan Abhiyaan (National Nutrition Mission)

Comprehensive holistic nutrition support to prevent stunting, under-nutrition, and anemia in children (0-6 years), adolescent girls, and expectant mothers.

health_and_safety ENTITLEMENT SPECIFICS
  • • Supplementary Nutrition Program (SNP) Take-Home Rations (THR) via Anganwadi.
  • • Hot cooked meals daily for children 3-6 years at local centers.
  • • Periodic growth monitoring and therapeutic foods for SAM/MAM children.
folder_open ACCESS CRITERIA
Poshan Tracker Registration Mother's MCP Card Aadhaar (Mother or Father)
Trinetra Booth Aid: Poshan Tracker app registration support for illiterate parents and intervention in cases of ration diversion or Anganwadi exclusion.
Poshan Tracker Portal open_in_new support_agent Anganwadi Connect

Women Empowerment & Maternal Care Ministry of WCD

PM Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)

Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) maternity incentive of ₹5,000 to compensate for wage loss and encourage institutional delivery for safe maternal health.

currency_rupee DBT INSTALLMENT STRUCTURE
  • • 1st Installment (₹3,000): Early registration of pregnancy & ANC check.
  • • 2nd Installment (₹2,000): Child birth registration & 1st cycle vaccines.
  • • Additional ₹6,000 if second child is a girl under PMMVY 2.0.
folder_open REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
MCP Card Record Mother's Aadhaar DBT NPCI-Linked Bank Account
Trinetra Booth Aid: NPCI Aadhaar bank seeding verification to prevent payment bounces, and PMMVY online portal dossier submission.

Universal Health Security National Health Authority

Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)

World's largest government-funded healthcare assurance scheme offering ₹5,00,000 annual cashless secondary & tertiary hospitalization per family.

local_hospital BENEFIT DETAILS
  • • ₹5 Lakh/family/year cashless cover across 27,000+ empanelled hospitals.
  • • Pre-existing diseases covered from day one without waiting period.
  • • Diagnostic tests, surgeries, medicines, and post-discharge covered.

PM SVANidhi Micro-Credit Scheme

Collateral-free working capital micro-loans for street vendors and small informal hawkers to liberate them from predatory loan sharks.

payments LOAN TRANCHES & INCENTIVES

  • • 7% Interest subsidy credited directly via DBT on timely repayment.
  • • Monthly cash back incentive on digital transactions (QR payments).
