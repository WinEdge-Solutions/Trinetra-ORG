Answers & Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

How is my donation utilized, and what are the overhead ratios?

100% of individual citizen contributions are funneled directly into bridge schooling fees, learning kits, nutrition, and immediate rescue rehabilitation. All administrative salaries, office utilities, and audit fees are independently funded by separate institutional CSR endowments.

Can I report child labour or child distress completely anonymously?

Yes. When using our Immediate Protection portal, the "Submit Anonymously" option scrubs personal identifiers and IP telemetry. The report is triaged confidentially by licensed caseworkers and coordinated with Childline 1098 and the Child Welfare Committee (CWC).

Are contributions eligible for tax exemption?

Tax exemption verification pending statutory filing. Official contribution receipts are issued for all donations in accordance with Section 8 non-profit accounting and Ministry of Corporate Affairs regulations.

Do you assist underprivileged families directly with Government Scheme paperwork?

Yes. Our 85+ grassroots community guidance booths across Rajasthan feature trained paralegal volunteers who assist illiterate parents in procuring birth certificates, Aadhaar cards, and applying for statutory programs like RTE Section 12(1)(c) and Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana completely free of charge.
