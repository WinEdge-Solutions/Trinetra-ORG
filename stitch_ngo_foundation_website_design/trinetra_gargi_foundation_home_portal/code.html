Answers & Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

How is my donation utilized, and what are the overhead ratios?

100% of individual public contributions are directly funneled into schooling fees, bridge school infrastructure, student kits, and nutrition. Our management, operational overhead, and compliance legal costs are separately covered through specific CSR institutional endowments.

Can I report child labour or child abuse completely anonymously?

Yes. When you use our Urgent Help & Report Portal, checking "Submit Anonymously" scrubs all metadata and personal IP identifiers. The report is passed directly to our rapid response field team and Childline 1098 without revealing your identity.

How do I claim my 80G tax deduction?

Upon successful payment, our automated portal issues an official Statutory Donation Accounting Receipt bearing our registration number directly to your registered email. In accordance with the IT Department rules, Trinetra Gargi Foundation files Form 10BD annually so your donation automatically reflects in your Annual Information Statement (AIS).

Do you assist families directly with Government Scheme paperwork?

Yes! Our 85+ grassroots community guidance booths feature trained paralegal volunteers who assist illiterate parents in procuring Aadhaar cards, setting up zero-balance Jan Dhan bank accounts, and submitting online forms for schemes like RTE Section 12(1)(c) and Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana without middlemen fees.
