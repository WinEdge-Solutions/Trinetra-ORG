From Yamuna Khadar Brick Kiln to State Board Topper: Sunita's 5-Year Journey

Found at age 11 carrying unfired clay bricks under informal bonded labor conditions along the floodplains, Sunita faced generational educational deprivation. Through coordinated enforcement with local authorities and our continuous Alwar Bridge learning pod, her holistic rehabilitation began.

timeline 5-Year Multi-Tier Intervention Trajectory

2019: Extraction Child Welfare Committee (CWC) clearance & nutritional stabilization.
2020-21: Bridge Node Foundational Hindi literacy, numeracy & trauma counseling.
2022: Formal Enrolment Admitted into Govt. Model Senior Secondary with TGF remedial tutors.
2024: 93.6% State Board Topper in commerce stream; ongoing college scholarship transition.

Breaking Generational Poverty: 24 Mothers Establish Self-Sustaining Tailoring Cooperative

In the urban informal clusters of Bhopal, seasonal wage uncertainty repeatedly forced female heads of household into exploitative unorganized daily labor. TGF mobilised 24 mothers, organized them into registered Self-Help Groups (SHGs), facilitated micro-credit linkages through State Rural Livelihoods Mission (SRLM), and established an electric tailoring workshop.

₹18,400 Avg. Monthly Rev/Member

Mohan's First Code: How a Ragpicker's Son Landed an IT Apprenticeship

Spending his formative years sorting scrap materials at the Ghazipur landfill border, 17-year-old Mohan enrolled in TGF's evening digital literacy incubator. Progressing through foundational Python syntax and database maintenance modules under dedicated industry volunteer mentorship, Mohan cleared technical assessments for an accredited junior hardware apprenticeship.

Rescued from Zari Workshops: Reintegrating 14 Children in Sitapur into Model Primary Schools

Operating inside poorly ventilated, unmapped home-basements, 14 children aged 8 to 13 were subjected to 13-hour shifts doing intricate embroidery work. In collaboration with the Anti-Human Trafficking Unit (AHTU) and local Child Welfare Committees, TGF executed safe extraction, facilitated medical trauma screening, and secured full admission into Kasturba Gandhi Balika Vidyalaya (KGBV) and State Model Schools.
