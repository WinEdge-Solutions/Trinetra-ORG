## Brand & Style

This design system expresses compassionate authority, vibrant human warmth, and structural clarity for a civic development and child welfare foundation. The aesthetic marries an uplifting civic ethos with restrained modern minimalism, ensuring transparency, dignity, and immediate emotional connection without decorative clutter.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Luminosity and Dignity:** Interfaces feel sunlit, open, and deliberate. Light backgrounds utilize soft ivory and warm cream tones instead of sterile clinic whites.
- **Graphic Authenticity:** In lieu of sensationalist or synthetic photography, the visual language relies strictly on bespoke geometric vector illustrations, precise typographic scale, authentic data displays, and crisp iconography.
- **Urgent Clarity & Trust:** High contrast Navy text against warm backdrops instills institutional reliability, while glowing marigold and terracotta accents celebrate empowerment, agency, and hope.
- **Humanist Tactility:** Surfaces rely on soft micro-borders, delicate tonal fills, and gentle curves to create welcoming, responsive tap targets.

## Colors

The palette balances joyful kinetic energy with dependable civic gravitas. 

- **Primary (`#D97706` / `#F59E0B`):** Warm Saffron Gold and Marigold evoke morning sun, grassroots optimism, and civic education. Used for primary conversion triggers, badges, and focal stats.
- **Secondary (`#0F2942`):** Deep Trust Navy anchors the institutional framework. Applied to headings, metric numerals, legal disclosures, and primary structural chrome.
- **Tertiary (`#059669` / `#10B981`):** Growth Sage represents renewal, nutrition, rehabilitation, and child protection workflows.
- **Emergency Action (`#DC2626`):** Reserved strictly for crisis intervention ("Help & Report Child Abuse") and immediate escalation hotlines.
- **Surface Architecture:** Default canvas is anchored on Warm Ivory (`#FAFAF9`), with elevated components utilizing Pearl White (`#FFFFFF`) or Warm Muted Ash (`#F5F5F4`), framed with subtle highlight amber borders (`#FEF3C7`).

## Typography

Typography relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies to synthesize friendliness, contemporary digital precision, and unimpeachable legitimacy.

- **Impact Counters & Numerical Anchors:** Numerical data points leverage heavy weights (`800`) with tight tracking to act as self-standing structural elements in reports and summaries.
- **Headlines:** Tightly tracked headings establish authority and momentum without aggressive austerity.
- **Body & Legibility:** Body copy maintains an open `1.6` line-height ratio in Charcoal (`#1E293B`) to ensure fatigue-free reading across programmatic mission briefs, financial disclosures, and scheme breakdowns.
- **Eyebrows & Badges:** `label-caps` in uppercase with wider tracking (`0.08em`) should sit above key section titles to contextualize program categories cleanly.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a disciplined 12-column fluid grid system bounded by a max container width of `1280px` to maintain optimal line lengths and structural composure.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Margin Rules
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` outer canvas margin and `1rem` gutter. Stack interactive scheme cards, metrics, and multi-tier action buttons vertically.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margin and `1.5rem` gutter. Metrics align 2x2; navigation consolidates into an off-canvas drawer with an exposed sticky emergency ribbon.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout with `3rem` canvas margin and `2rem` gutter. Dual-tier header structures full taxonomy, while report tools and impact grids operate in balanced multi-column matrices.

### Spacing Cadence
Component internals use strict `space-sm` (`8px`) and `space-md` (`16px`) padding metrics. Section dividers breathe with `space-xl` (`40px`) or multiples thereof to prevent cognitive saturation.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain modern purity and programmatic transparency, this design system avoids heavy drop shadows and synthetic skewing. Hierarchy is expressed through **tonal stacking, subtle warm borders, and refined micro-elevations**.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Base Warm Ivory (`#FAFAF9`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Warm Muted Card (`#F5F5F4`) resting directly on canvas, defined by a 1px solid border in `#FEF3C7` or `#E2E8F0`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Floating Elements):** Active cards and sticky headers utilize an ultra-diffused, ambient warm shadow: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Emergency Drawers):** High-priority overlays use an assertive backdrop tint (`rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`) and a subtle boundary line.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 2 (Rounded)** geometries. 

- Interactive buttons, form inputs, and metric containers feature `0.5rem` (8px) corner radii.
- Content cards, scheme overview blocks, and modular program containers employ `1rem` (16px) radii (`rounded-lg`).
- Feature banners, stat highlight panels, and modal shells use `1.5rem` (24px) radii (`rounded-xl`).
- Category badges, status indicators, and quick-action trigger pills use full circular rounding (`9999px`) to maintain human approachability and contrast against rectangular content blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Donate / Support):** Gradient or solid Saffron Gold (`#D97706`), text in `#FFFFFF`, font-weight `600`, padding `0.75rem 1.75rem`, rounded `0.5rem`. Subtle hover shift to `#B45309`.
- **Secondary Civic Action (Get Involved / Schemes):** Pure White surface, 1.5px border in Trust Navy (`#0F2942`), text in `#0F2942`.
- **Emergency Action Bar Button (Help & Report):** Emergency Crimson (`#DC2626`) fill, text in `#FFFFFF`, accompanying alert icon. Immediate visual priority.

### Navigation Systems
- **Top Utility & Emergency Ribbon:** Persistent sticky bar above header featuring immediate distress line ("Childline 1098 / Rapid Protection Hotline") in deep navy with bold coral/white phone indicators.
- **Primary Sticky Navigation Bar:** Clean ivory header with prominent foundation brandmark, primary routing (Home, About Us, Our Work, Government Schemes, Help & Report, Impact, Stories, Volunteer), and an anchored gold "Donate" pill.

### Impact Counters
- Monolithic numerical counters displayed in Navy (`#0F2942`) or Marigold (`#D97706`) using `stat-metric`. Subtitles in Slate (`#64748B`) using `body-sm`. Bordered in soft amber with generous padding (`space-lg`).

### Government Scheme Cards
- Structured information architecture: Category pill tag at top left, clear headline (`headline-sm`), key eligibility criteria checklist using green check SVG indicators, application deadline badge, and an actionable "View Scheme & Apply" link with trailing arrow.

### Trust Badges & Verification Seals
- Strict monochromatic or duotone vector badges (e.g., "Section 8 Registered Non-Profit", "GuideStar Platinum Transparency", "National CSR Awardee"). High-contrast slate or gold vector emblems with micro typography, providing legitimacy without commercial clutter.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background in `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#1E293B`, placeholder `#94A3B8`. Focus state applies a `2px` ring in `#D97706` with an offset of `1px`.
