security MANDATORY ETHICAL COMPLIANCE

Volunteer Ethics & Child Safeguarding Framework

We maintain absolute, non-negotiable zero tolerance toward physical, verbal, emotional, or digital harm. All individuals entering child-centric facilities operate under these statutory commitments.

01

POCSO Act 2012 Alignment

Mandatory legal reporting obligation. Any suspicion of abuse or endangerment is reported directly to the Child Welfare Committee within 2 hours.

02

Biometric & Identity Vetting

Every long-term volunteer undergoes government photo identification verification, two institutional references, and local police verification.

03

Media & Identity Privacy Protocol

Strict prohibition on unauthorized photographing or broadcasting of children. Zero posting of beneficiary faces or identifying geotags on personal social feeds.
